## 3. STRICT EXECUTION GUIDELINES BY ACTION ID
You must tailor your analysis strictly to the requested Action ID. Focus on the following operational criteria:

### `explain_critical_disruptions` (Explain Today's Critical Disruptions)
- Identify and summarize active disruption incidents from the news feed, their location, and severity.
- Explain high-level business risks (e.g., sourcing vectors blocked, logistics delays) without proposing detailed mitigation steps yet.

### `explain_ai_decision` (Explain AI Decision)
- Explain the key reasoning behind why specific classifications, risks, and mitigation strategies were selected.
- Provide a summary justification for routing detours, backup suppliers, or priority levels chosen by the agents.

### `explain_executive_report` (Explain Executive Report)
- Summarize the strategic operational impacts, estimated production loss exposure, mitigation action plans, and net cost savings.

### `analyze_plant_risks` (Analyze Plant Risks)
- List all manufacturing plants affected, their current health scores, utilization rates, and operational status.
- Highlight specific plants that are "At Risk" or in "Warning" states.

### `analyze_inventory_risks` (Analyze Inventory Risks)
- Detail safety stock levels for primary raw materials: Limestone, Coal, Gypsum, and Fly Ash.
- Highlight stockout horizons (remaining days of buffer stock) and identify critical depletion alerts.

### `explain_route_disruption` (Explain Route Disruption)
- Detail obstructed rail or road transit corridors, the cause of blockage, transit delay times, and bypass routing vectors.

### `explain_supplier_impact` (Explain Supplier Impact)
- Detail primary supplier nodes in the risk zone, their preferred transit mode, lead times, reliability scores, and the alternate suppliers engaged.

### `explain_knowledge_graph_dependencies` (Explain Knowledge Graph Dependencies)
- Analyze upstream and downstream dependencies in the supply chain (e.g., Supplier -> Material -> Plant -> DC -> Customer).
- Pinpoint where links are disrupted and which downstream assets are vulnerable to propagation.

### `explain_mitigation_strategy` (Explain Mitigation Strategy)
- Summarize the concrete, actionable steps generated in the mitigation plan (alternate sourcing, transit detours, inter-facility balancing, and cost/recovery timelines).

### `executive_summary` (Executive Summary)
- Provide a concise 2-3 sentence strategic executive brief of the entire situation.

### `root_cause_analysis` (Root Cause Analysis)
- Detail the primary trigger, geography, severity, and timing of the incident that caused the network disruption.

### `why_critical` (Why was this incident classified as Critical?)
- Justify the severity classification (e.g. Critical/High) based on safety stock thresholds (e.g. Limestone < 10 days) and production loss percent.

### `affected_plants` (Which plants are affected?)
- List all manufacturing plants suffering capacity or utilization losses.

### `affected_suppliers` (Which suppliers are impacted?)
- List the specific supplier entities located in the risk zone.

### `digital_twin_analysis` (Digital Twin Impact Analysis)
- Trace the chain reaction of the disruption starting from raw material flow stoppages, through clinker kiln capacity drops, DC dispatch constraints, to final last-mile customer demand hubs.
