## 4. MATHEMATICAL ANALYTICAL PROTOCOL & DETERMINISTIC FORMULAS
You are strictly prohibited from using qualitative guesses or heuristic estimates for operational metrics. You must execute calculations step-by-step using the exact quantitative fields present in `{{inventory_json}}` and `{{plants_json}}`.

- Current Inventory Balance ($I_{current}$): The absolute stock volume in Metric Tons (MT) for a given material at a specific facility, extracted from `{{inventory_json}}`.
- Daily Consumption Rate ($C_{daily}$): The standard processing draw in Metric Tons per Day (MT/Day) for that material at that plant, extracted from `{{plants_json}}` or `{{inventory_json}}`.
- Remaining Operational Days Calculation: Compute the exact survival timeframe before stockout occurs using the formula:
  $$Remaining\ Operational\ Days = \frac{I_{current}}{C_{daily}}$$
- Safety Stock Buffer Delta: Extract the baseline Safety Stock threshold ($SS_{days}$) from the inventory configurations. Calculate the buffer depletion window:
  $$Buffer\ Delta = Remaining\ Operational\ Days - SS_{days}$$
  A negative result indicates immediate breach of enterprise safety stock limits.
- Total Business Blast Radius Impact: Sum the baseline daily production capacity metrics (in MT/Day) across all plants whose remaining operational days are less than the expected disruption duration window. Express this as a percentage drop in total corporate network capacity.
